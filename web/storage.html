Offline & storage

A device copy makes an index searchable offline and much faster. Its reverse sidecar keeps ends-with searches (.*tion) fast on the copy — without it they still work, just slowly. Cached pieces are the fragments streamed searches fetched as they went; they speed up repeat queries and are safe to clear any time.

Puzzle datasets

Rhymes, meanings, categories and lists are separate small files, fetched the first time a query needs them. Store them all now and every construct keeps working offline — the whole set is under 10 MB.

    Together, a device copy + its sidecar + the datasets are a complete offline puzzle kit — install the site as an app and it keeps working with no connection at all.
